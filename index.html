📖 Student Guide & Directions

Welcome! Use your knowledge of linear inequalities, absolute value, interval notation, and logic bounds to locate and sink all hidden enemy ships.

📋 How to Play

  • 1. Select Target: Click any cell (A1–G7) on the $7 \times 7$ grid on the left to launch a torpedo.
  • 2. Solve the Problem: The inequality or word problem for that coordinate will appear on the right side.
  • 3. Evaluate Shot:
    • 💥 HIT: Correct math calculation and an enemy ship segment is hit!
    • 🌊 MISS: Correct math calculation, but the shot landed in open water.
    • ⚠️ MISFIRE: Incorrect answer. Read the step-by-step solution to learn!
  • 4. Victory: Sink all 3 enemy vessels (8 hits total) to win.

💡 Review Tips

  • 📝 Scratch Paper: Keep paper handy for multi-step boundary problems.
  • ⚠️ Sign Flips: Remember to flip inequality signs when multiplying or dividing by a negative number.
  • 🔍 Intervals: Use ( ) for strict ($<, >$) and [ ] for inclusive ($\le, \ge$).
  • 🔗 Compound Logic: AND needs overlap (intersection); OR combines sets (union).
